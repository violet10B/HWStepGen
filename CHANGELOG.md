Anything under `src/hwsg/detail/` is private and may change in a minor
release.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-07-30

Initial release.

- `hwsg::Stepper`: velocity control for step/dir drivers with the pulse train
  generated by the LEDC peripheral
- `hwsg::Ramp`: non-blocking acceleration with Linear, SmoothStep,
  SmootherStep and Sinusoidal easing
- `hwsg::StepperBank`: group operations, including `stopAll()`
- Supports ESP32, S2, S3, C3, C6, H2 and P4 on Arduino core 2.x and 3.x
- Automatic LEDC channel allocation, one channel per timer
- Microstepping-aware speed conversion, ENABLE pin control, direction
  inversion, signed speeds
- Pin validation against the running chip; all failures reported as
  `hwsg::Error` values
